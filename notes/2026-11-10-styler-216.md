# Windhawk Enhanced Styler 2.1.6: section arrow, settings badge, tabs dock

Date: 2026-11-10. Mod `local@windhawk-enhanced-styler`, 2.1.5 to 2.1.6.

## What changed

- Section collapse arrow: a 20px dark #181825 box with a 2px #45475a edge inside the card padding
  (was a 12px box the rotated arrow poked out of, partly clipped by the card edge). Arrow 9px,
  centred. Blue to mauve edge on hover. Open sections still hide it until hover (stock).
- "N settings" badge on a collapsed section: dark chip, 2px grey edge, gradient edge on hover.
- Mod page tabs (Details, Settings, Source code, Changelog, Advanced): one #1e1e2e pill with 2px
  dividers, no ink underline, no grey rule under the bar. Hover fills a tab with a soft blue to
  mauve wash and turns the pill edge to the gradient; the active tab is bold blue on #181825.

## Registry

- `HKLM\SOFTWARE\Windhawk\Engine\Mods\local@windhawk-enhanced-styler` `LibraryFileName`
- Old: `local@windhawk-enhanced-styler_2.1.5_polish.dll`
- New: `local@windhawk-enhanced-styler_2.1.6_tabs.dll`

## Install / rollback

`scripts\2026-11-10-styler-216-install.ps1 -Action WaitInstall` (elevated) installs when the
Windhawk window closes. Rollback with the window closed: `-Action Rollback` (imports
`backups\2026-11-10-styler-216\mod-key.reg`, restores `live.wh.cpp`).
