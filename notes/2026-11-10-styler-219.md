# Windhawk Enhanced Styler 2.1.9: theme switch as a pill dock

Date: 2026-11-10. Mod `local@windhawk-enhanced-styler`, 2.1.7 (live) to 2.1.9. 2.1.8 was built but
never installed; its watcher was stopped and 2.1.9 carries all of it (see `2026-11-10-styler-218.md`).

## What changed

- Every segmented switch, such as Theme (Dark, Light, System) on the app settings page, is now one
  pill dock like the mod page tabs: Mocha base fill (was a light grey), 2px grey dividers between
  items, 2px grey edge at rest, blue to mauve edge on hover, hovered item gets a soft blue to mauve
  wash, chosen item is blue bold text on mantle. The sliding light thumb is gone.
- The Visual / Textual switch on a mod's settings tab keeps its own rules (excluded by selector).

Rules: `work\2026-11-10-styler-segmented\seg.css`. Proof (live window, forced hover):
`seg-rest.png`, `seg-hover.png` in that folder.

## Registry

- `HKLM\SOFTWARE\Windhawk\Engine\Mods\local@windhawk-enhanced-styler` `LibraryFileName`
- Old: `local@windhawk-enhanced-styler_2.1.7_dialogs.dll`
- New: `local@windhawk-enhanced-styler_2.1.9_settings.dll`

## Install / rollback

- Backup: `backups\2026-11-10-styler-219\mod-key.reg`, `live.wh.cpp`.
- `scripts\2026-11-10-styler-219-install.ps1 -Action WaitInstall` installs when the Windhawk
  window closes. Rollback: same script, `-Action Rollback`, elevated, window closed.
