## 1:05 am (Nov 10): group "..." menu no longer hidden behind the panel (Styler Plus 1.8.4)

- Cause: the group "..." menu is its own popup window ("PopupHost") opened to the left of the
  panel. Both sit in the same shell z-band; when the panel window moves up the z-order (a real
  click activates it), it covers the ~270 px of the menu that overlaps the panel.
- Fix, fork 1.8.4 (`work\nc-hover-plus`, source + DLL backed up as `.bak-1.8.3`): subclasses the
  panel CoreWindow on its own thread; after every z-order change (WM_WINDOWPOSCHANGED without
  SWP_NOZORDER) it puts that thread's visible Xaml_WindowedPopupClass windows back on top
