# Windhawk Styler 2.0.48 to 2.0.52: floating bar, pills, bottom clearance (2026-10-13)

Owner reports: the save bar scrolls away (it used to float), the page card loses its rounding mid-scroll and clips it at the bottom, and the mode/version switches plus the mod id chip should join into pills.

## Causes (all measured in the live UI)

- The save bar (`SaveSettingsCard`) scrolled with the page because the mod forced `position: relative` on it, overriding the interface's own `position: sticky; top: 0` (styled-components `.gxTCKZ`). First fix attempt only removed it from the relative-surface list, but the card also matches `.ant-card`, so relative still won. 2.0.49 scopes that rule to `.ant-card:not([class*="SaveSettingsCard"])`.
- Stuck at top 0, the bar's rounded top touched the viewport edge and read as cut. 2.0.51 sticks it 12px down (measured 12px offset at 600px scroll), matching the 12px side insets.
- The page card ends exactly at the scroll viewport bottom (measured 0px gap), slicing its bottom curve. The scroller leaves the card's bottom margin out of its scroll range, so 2.0.50 pads the page container 12px instead (measured 12px gap at max scroll on a 1860px page).
- The Visual/Textual items were 2px/0px and the version radios 2px/0px; the mod id chip (`CardTitleModId`) was 2px. 2.0.48 rounds outer ends only: segments 14px/0, radios 16px/0, chip 16px.
- 2.0.52 is a readme-line-only release so the installed source matches the repo.

## Verified live (no probe present for the final numbers)

- Save card computed `position: sticky`; stuck 12px below the scroll top; 16px radius; floating-pill screenshots at mid and bottom scroll.
- Scroll range grew by the 12px container padding; card bottom clears the edge.
- Segment, radio and chip radii as above; accent variables unchanged.
