# Windhawk Enhanced Styler 2.1.8: reset pills, number arrows, changed-setting bars

Date: 2026-11-10. Mod `local@windhawk-enhanced-styler`, 2.1.7 to 2.1.8.

## What changed (mod settings page)

- "Reset to default" pill: crust fill (#11111b), 2px grey edge (#45475a) at rest, blue to mauve
  edge on hover and keyboard focus, mantle fill on hover, base fill when pressed.
- Number box up/down arrows: one small pill (crust fill, 2px grey edge, blue to mauve edge on
  hover). Each arrow gets a soft blue wash on hover, mauve when pressed; disabled arrows go dim.
- The bar beside each changed setting: blue to mauve, top to bottom (was flat blue).

CSS block. Proof (forced hover, live window): `r-rest.png`, `r-hover.png`, `n-hover.png`,
