# Changelog

What changed in each Seamless Workshop release. The Workshop checks for a new version once a day and offers it on its Updates page.

## 1.4.0

- DavidHiFi's whole setup in one go, on the Updates page and the welcome card: every mod he runs, his own and the Windhawk library ones.
- "Copy everything, settings included" puts each mod on or off the way he has it, with his settings and app lists. Nothing private is copied.
- "Install everything, default settings" installs them all turned off with each mod's own settings.
- "Clean slate first" (on by default) takes your current mods off first. They are backed up and saved as a Before preset.
- One mod that fails no longer stops the rest. The window shows the step it is on and names what did not work.

## 1.3.0

- DavidHiFi's mods are sorted into groups on every list: Taskbar, Explorer, Windows and menus, Tools and Fixes, in that order. A mod with no group shows under Other.
- The Mods page sorts by group by default. "Where it comes from" and "On or off" are still in Sort into.
- Search still looks through every group, and typing a group name finds all of its mods.

## 1.2.0

- DavidHiFi's mods come first in Marketplace, with a count of how many he has and how many you have. Clicking one you already have opens it.
- Updates show what changed before you install them: on the Updates page, on the mod's card and in the tray message.
- A welcome on first open: apply his whole setup in one click, or pick from his mods.
- "Remove all of DavidHiFi's mods" on the Updates page takes them all off in one go. Their settings are saved as a preset first, and your other mods stay.
- Backups now cover any number of mods, so updating or removing more than 32 at once works.

## 1.1.1

- The mod's page in Windhawk shows a screenshot.

## 1.1.0

- Mods and Marketplace show every mod as a card, in a grid or a list. Switch between them at the top of the page.
- Search, Show and Sort sit above the list and work as you type or pick.
- Optional "Show on Discord" setting, off by default.
- The tray icon has its own id, so pinning it no longer moves the icons of other Seamless mods.
- Plainer names and descriptions on the Settings page.

## 1.0.0

- First release: one window inside Windhawk to install, update, turn on and off, and set up every DavidHiFi mod.
- Updates show up within a day and install after a checksum check.
- DavidHiFi's settings for one mod, or his whole setup in one go. Your own settings are saved as a preset first.
