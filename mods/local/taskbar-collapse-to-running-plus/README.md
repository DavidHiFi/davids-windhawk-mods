# Taskbar Collapse to Running Plus

Hide pinned apps that are not running. Hover over the taskbar to reveal them with a slide and fade.

![Taskbar dock preview](../../../media/previews/taskbar-collapse-to-running-plus.png)

Use the mod settings to choose hover, empty-taskbar click, or a hotkey to reveal apps.

Version 1.1.5 fades appearing and disappearing icons faster while they overlap. With Taskbar System Info Plus 1.4.17, the apps dock outline follows the transition without one-frame width flashes.

Based on [Taskbar collapse to running apps](https://windhawk.net/mods/taskbar-collapse-to-running) by [Lars](https://github.com/LarsGudm). MIT license.
