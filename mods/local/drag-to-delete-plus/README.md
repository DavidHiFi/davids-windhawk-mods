# Drag to Delete Plus

[? Support on Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/DavidHiFi)

A floating trash target appears while you drag files, so you can drop them
straight into the Recycle Bin. Five background finishes, circle or square
shapes, and full color control, in Catppuccin Mocha with a blue accent out of
the box.

![Drag to Delete Plus preview](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DavidHiFi/davids-windhawk-mods/main/media/previews/drag-to-delete-plus.png)

## Features

[? Support on Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/DavidHiFi)

- **Five background finishes.** Gradient glass, solid, acrylic or mica blur of
  what is behind the target, or no background at all.
- **Circle, rounded square or square target** with adjustable corner radius,
  border width, shadow spread and overall size.
- **Every color is yours.** Background (gradient top, bottom or solid), border,
  trash icon, shadow and hover tint, each a 0xAARRGGBB value with alpha.
- **Red hover cue.** The target tints toward red while your files are over it
  and flashes on a successful drop. Turn it off or pick another color.
- **Accepts copy-only drags.** Files pulled from archives or apps that only
  offer copying can be recycled too.
- **Top or bottom placement**, per-monitor DPI aware.

## How to use

[? Support on Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/DavidHiFi)

Drag any file or folder and the target fades in. Drop the files on it to move
them to the Recycle Bin, or let go anywhere else to cancel. Disable the
original Drag to Delete before enabling this mod.

Acrylic and mica blur the screen behind the target when a drag starts; the mod
renders them itself, so they work in the transparent overlay without any system
backdrop.
